Questions people would definitely ask

Wait, is this a real product?

No. Candulo is a parody concept built for a class project. Babies grow their own teeth on a perfectly good schedule, and no dentist anywhere recommends dentures for infants.

Can I actually buy a set?

You cannot. There is no checkout, no warehouse, and no factory — and as you may have noticed, the waitlist field doesn't exactly want to be filled in either.

Why does the pricing go up to $7,499.99?

Because "a smile for everyone" sounds a lot funnier next to a price only a few people could ever pay.

What age is Candulo "for"?

Zero to twelve months, which is exactly the range where a full set of dentures makes the least sense — that's the joke.
